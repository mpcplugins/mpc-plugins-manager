- **More from the same maker, right on the plugin's page.** Open a plugin's page and roll your finger up: the page
  scrolls down into **MORE FROM <maker>**, every other plugin by that maker as pictures with names, 8 at a time.
  Keep rolling for the rest; roll back down for the top of the page. Tap a picture to open its page. Under WHAT'S NEW
  the page says how many more there are.

  whole-screen redraws while you roll).

**Install:** download `MPC-Plugins-Manager-1.5.1-mpc-sdk.zip`, unzip it, copy the folder `The Disk King - VST - MPC Plugins Manager` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC, restart, add a Plugin track and pick External Plugins > The Disk King > MPC Plugins Manager, then tap the arrow next to its name. Needs MPC OS 3.10.1 with Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support on. Full guide: https://thediskking.com/plugins/install/
