- **Maker pages.** Tap a maker's name (on a plugin's card or its details page) and the list shows **everything by
  that maker**, every tab at once, scrollable like the normal list. The TYPE pill shows "By <maker>"; tap it to go
  back. The phone page does the same: tap a maker's name, "Show all" goes back.

**Install:** download `MPC-Plugins-Manager-1.5.0-mpc-sdk.zip`, unzip it, copy the folder `The Disk King - VST - MPC Plugins Manager` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC, restart, add a Plugin track and pick External Plugins > The Disk King > MPC Plugins Manager, then tap the arrow next to its name. Needs MPC OS 3.10.1 with Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support on. Full guide: https://thediskking.com/plugins/install/
