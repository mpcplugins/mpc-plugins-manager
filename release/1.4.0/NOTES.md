**Install:** download `MPC-Plugins-Manager-1.4.0-mpc-sdk.zip`, unzip it, copy the folder `The Disk King - VST - MPC Plugins Manager` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC, restart, add a Plugin track and pick External Plugins > The Disk King > MPC Plugins Manager, then tap the arrow next to its name. Needs MPC OS 3.10.1 with Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support on. Full guide: https://thediskking.com/plugins/install/
